About Bulk URL Opener

About Bulk URL Opener

urlbulkopener.com is a free online bulk URL opener. You give it a list of web addresses, and it opens them in new tabs so you don't have to do it yourself, one link at a time. It runs entirely in the browser, so there is nothing to install.

Why We Created This Tool

Picture a list of sixty links. Opening them by hand means sixty rounds of copy, switch tab, paste, press Enter. It isn't hard, but it eats a surprising amount of time, and it's easy to lose your place halfway through.

That small, repetitive chore is why we built this tool. We wanted a page that does one thing well and doesn't get in the way, so the time you save goes into reading the pages instead of opening them.

What Bulk URL Opener Offers

In short, the tool takes your list, tidies it, and opens it. It reads links you paste or import from a file, flags entries that aren't valid web addresses or that appear twice, and then opens the valid ones together. The full list of features, supported file types and limits is on the homepage, next to the tool itself.

Who Can Benefit From It

Anyone who works from a list of links, whether or not they're technical.

  • SEO Professionals

    Checking redirects after a site migration, or going through the URLs from an audit.

  • Developers and Testers

    Opening a batch of staging or test pages to see which ones fail to load.

  • Marketers and Website Owners

    Reviewing landing pages and campaign links before they go live.

  • Researchers and Students

    Opening a saved list of sources together for reading and comparison.

Our Focus on Simplicity and Usability

We try to keep the tool small. Every extra button is one more thing to figure out, so we only add what helps with the core job. We also try to be honest about what the tool can't control, such as how your browser handles several new tabs at once. Those notes sit right on the homepage, where you need them.

Privacy and Responsible Use

Privacy

The links you paste and the files you import are processed in your browser and are not uploaded to a server by the tool. How the rest of the website handles information is covered in the Privacy Policy.

Responsible use

The tool opens whatever you give it and can't judge whether a page is safe, so stick to lists from sources you trust. Please use it lawfully and in line with our Terms and Conditions.
