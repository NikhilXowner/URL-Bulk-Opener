Information the tool processes

The URLs and files you put into the tool are handled in your browser. Section 3 explains exactly how.

URLs and files you use

Text you paste, and TXT, CSV, Excel (.xls, .xlsx) and Word (.docx) files you import, are read and checked by code running in your own browser. The tool’s code does not send your list or the contents of your files to our server or to any other service, and it does not save them to cookies or browser storage. Your list stays on the page until you clear it, reload the page or close the tab.

Two things are worth knowing:

  • Excel and Word imports load helper libraries. The first time you import one of these formats, your browser downloads a reading library from a public content delivery network (SheetJS, Cloudflare’s cdnjs or jsDelivr). Those providers can see an ordinary request from your browser, including your IP address. They do not receive your file contents.
  • Opening links contacts the destination sites. When you choose Open All Valid URLs, your browser visits each address in a new tab. Those websites can see your visit and may see that you arrived from urlbulkopener.com, depending on your browser settings. Their own privacy policies apply, and we do not control them.

Because your list is not sent to us, we cannot see it, recover it or delete it for you. Browser extensions, shared devices and your browser’s own history or autofill can still keep copies, so avoid entering links that contain private tokens on a computer you do not control.

How information is used

  • To deliver, operate and maintain the website and tool.
  • To find and fix errors, and to understand which devices and browsers need support.
  • To read and reply to messages sent through the contact form.
  • To protect the site against abuse, spam and security threats.
  • To meet legal obligations, and to establish or defend legal claims where necessary.

We do not use the contents of your URL lists for any purpose, because we do not receive them.
