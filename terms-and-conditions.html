What the tool does

The tool lets you paste web addresses, or import them from TXT, CSV, Excel (.xls, .xlsx) and Word (.docx) files, then review them and open the valid ones in new browser tabs when you click the button. It finds and flags duplicates, and it accepts only http and https addresses. If you leave off the protocol, it assumes https for anything that looks like a real domain. Other schemes, such as javascript: or ftp:, are rejected. Each import is limited to 10 MB and 5,000 unique URLs. The tool is free to use.

Its checks are about format only. The tool does not visit a link, so it cannot tell you whether a page exists, is safe, is accurate or will open the way you expect. We may change, add or remove features at any time.

Technical limitations

  • Pop-up blocking: browsers often limit or block many tabs opened at once. The tool reports how many were blocked, and you may need to allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

  • Destinations: a link may download a file instead of opening a page, redirect elsewhere, ask you to sign in or be unavailable. That depends on the destination site, not the tool.
